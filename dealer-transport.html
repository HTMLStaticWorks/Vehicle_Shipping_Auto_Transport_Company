Dealer Inventory Movement & Fleet Logistics

Auto dealerships require predictable inventory turnover. When cars sit at auctions or remote distribution yards, holding costs accrue and sales opportunities are delayed. RoadLink Auto pairs dealership management teams with a dedicated commercial dispatch coordinator.

Whether shifting a single customer order across state lines or dispatching full 9-car transport loads from an auto auction to your showroom floor, we maintain strict scheduling compliance.

Multi-Vehicle Batch Hauling

Full truckload (FTL) and less-than-truckload (LTL) pricing discounts tailored for dealership lot balancing and multi-unit transfers.

Auction Gate Retrieval

Direct coordination with Manheim, Copart, IAAI, and Adesa facilities. We validate buyer numbers and PIN codes to prevent storage penalties.

Dealer Trades & Swaps

Fast inter-dealership trades allowing your sales desk to locate and deliver specific customer trim packages within 24 to 48 hours.

Direct Customer Delivery

Support for modern online vehicle purchases with white-glove doorstep delivery to out-of-state buyers with full photographic condition sign-off.

Dedicated B2B Dispatch & Billing

  • Centralized Billing Accounts: Consolidated monthly electronic invoicing and clear Bill of Lading documentation for every VIN.
