Open vs. Enclosed Transport

Compare our two primary carrier carrier architectures to find the ideal protection tier for your automobile.

Multi-Car Open Rig

Open Auto Transport

Multi-vehicle double-decker carriers carrying 7-10 vehicles across highway freight lanes. Outstanding availability, cost-effectiveness, and rapid dispatch scheduling nationwide.

  • Most Economical: 35% to 50% lower rate than enclosed trailers.
  • Fastest Dispatch: Largest carrier network capacity nationwide.
  • Ideal For: Daily sedans, crossovers, SUVs, family vans & work trucks.
  • Full Cargo Insurance: Standard carrier insurance included.
Economy Standard Open Transport Details
Hard-Sided Enclosed Trailer

Enclosed Auto Transport

Rigid-walled climate-shielded trailers designed to eliminate road debris, dirt, UV radiation, and atmospheric moisture. Ideal for high-value and sensitive automobiles.

  • Complete Shielding: 100% barrier against rocks, gravel, weather & dust.
  • Hydraulic Lift-Gates: Horizontal loading prevents low-clearance scraping.
  • Ideal For: Exotics, vintage classics, supercars & luxury collector vehicles.
  • Expanded Policy Limits: Up to $1M+ white-glove cargo coverage.

Carrier Equipment & Safety Protocols

Every vehicle entrusted to RoadLink Auto is secured according to rigorous Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) cargo guidelines. We require verified equipment maintenance logs and over-the-tire soft nylon tie-downs.

Electronic Digital Bill of Lading (eBOL)

High-resolution condition photography and digital sign-off completed at both origin and destination.

Soft Nylon Wheel Straps (Zero Chassis Strain)

No metal hooks or chains attached to axles or suspension components. Only soft webbing around the tires.

Active Fleet Telematics & Corridor Checkpoints

Real-time corridor reporting keeps our dispatch and clients informed across all interstate transit miles.
