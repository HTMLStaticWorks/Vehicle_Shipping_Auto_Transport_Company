How Door-to-Door Transport Operates

Door-to-door vehicle transport represents the most comfortable auto shipping method available. Instead of driving your vehicle to a busy freight terminal and arranging costly rideshares home, the carrier coordinates pickup right outside your front door.

Upon reaching your destination city, the driver offloads the vehicle as close to your new home or office as local street dimensions and city zoning ordinances legally permit.

Street Clearance & Local Access Guidelines

Commercial auto haulers measure up to 75 to 80 feet in length and weigh up to 80,000 lbs when loaded. Due to their size, certain residential environments cannot safely accommodate direct access:

Low Tree Branches & Power Lines Overhead wires and low-hanging branches can damage vehicles on the upper transport deck.
Narrow Cul-de-Sacs & Dead Ends Carriers require adequate turning radius. Dead-end roads without turnabouts can trap large tractor-trailers.
Gated Communities with Weight Bans Many HOA communities prohibit commercial vehicles exceeding standard delivery van weights.
Nearby Meeting Point Alternative If access is restricted, the driver arranges a convenient meeting spot at a nearby grocery lot or wide highway pull-off.

Customer Communication & Arrival Protocol

Smooth door-to-door coordination relies on direct, proactive communication between driver, central dispatch, and client:

24-Hour Notice

Driver calls 1 day prior to confirm scheduled estimated arrival window.

2-Hour Real-Time ETA

Driver contacts you when 2 hours out to finalize street address clearance.

Joint Walkaround

Inspect vehicle, record odometer, review Bill of Lading, and hand over keys.
