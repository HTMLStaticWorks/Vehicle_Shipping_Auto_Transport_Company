Service Overview & Execution

Every RoadLink Auto shipment operates under standardized transport guidelines developed to balance efficiency, equipment reliability, and customer communication. Our carrier network utilizes specialized car haulers configured to protect vehicle chassis and finishes during interstate movement.

Whether shipping an everyday passenger sedan or high-value dealer stock, vehicles are secured using over-the-tire nylon soft straps rather than old-fashioned chain hooks. This prevents unnecessary tension on suspension geometry and eliminates underbody scratch risks.

What You Should Prepare Prior to Pickup

  • Wash the Exterior: A clean vehicle allows both you and the carrier driver to perform an accurate Bill of Lading walkaround inspection.
  • Maintain 1/4 Tank of Fuel: Keep approximately one-quarter tank of gasoline for loading and unloading. Excess fuel adds unnecessary trailer weight.
  • Remove Personal Items: Personal cargo is not covered under standard carrier transit insurance. Remove toll transponders and personal valuables.
  • Provide One Working Key: The driver requires one operational key or remote fob to steer and reposition the vehicle on the transport deck.
