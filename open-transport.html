What is Open Auto Transport?

Open auto transport utilizes specialized double-deck commercial carriers designed to transport between 7 and 10 vehicles simultaneously. This shared-capacity methodology significantly reduces per-vehicle fuel consumption and toll expenses, passing direct cost savings on to vehicle owners and dealerships.

While vehicles are transported on open decks exposed to ambient highway conditions (rain, sun, and road dust—comparable to driving on the interstate yourself), every vehicle is anchored securely with soft nylon tire webbing to eliminate structural strain.

When to Choose Open Transport

  • Standard Daily Vehicles: Sedans, hatchbacks, family SUVs, minivans, and stock pickup trucks.
  • Budget-Conscious Relocations: Maximize your relocation budget without compromising carrier insurance or tracking reliability.
  • Tight Booking Schedules: Because open carriers represent the overwhelming majority of operating rigs, pickup matching is faster than enclosed alternatives.
  • Dealer Lot Distribution: Multi-car movements between regional branches and auctions.

Preparing Your Vehicle for Open Carrier Shipping

Ensure an effortless pickup by following these essential vehicle preparation steps:

1. Exterior Wash: Clean off road dirt so existing paint chips or scratches can be clearly documented during the joint inspection.
2. Remove Valuables & Transponders: Take out all GPS units, toll passes (E-ZPass, FasTrak), and loose personal belongings.
3. Check Fluid Levels & Leaks: Ensure no major active fluid drips (oil, coolant, brake fluid) that could damage vehicles below.
4. Lower Antenna & Retract Mirrors: Fold in side-view mirrors and remove any aftermarket roof luggage carriers or ski racks.
