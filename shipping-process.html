Operational Workflow

The 6-Stage Vehicle Shipping Process

A step-by-step breakdown illustrating how RoadLink Auto coordinates dispatch, vehicle intake, transit tracking, and destination delivery with certified carriers nationwide.

View Prep Checklist Calculate Route Quote
Transparent 6-Stage Journey
01
Initiation

Request Quote & Define Corridor

Submit your origin and destination ZIP codes, vehicle make/model/year, and choose between Open or Enclosed carrier mode. Our dispatch algorithm calculates an instant, all-inclusive rate with zero hidden surcharges.

Deliverable: Instant price lock estimate with estimated transit timeframe (e.g. 3-5 business days).
02
Confirmation

Confirm Shipment Reservation

Review the route summary and confirm your preferred pickup date window. Your reservation is registered into central dispatch without requiring an upfront deposit until a verified carrier is formally routed.

Deliverable: Booking confirmation email with your unique consignment tracking number (e.g. RL-2026-04821).
03
Dispatch

Carrier Assignment & Vetting

Our compliance desk matches your vehicle with a screened, licensed carrier on your interstate corridor. We verify active FMCSA authority, safety ratings, and minimum $250k–$1M cargo insurance certificate before dispatch.

Deliverable: Assigned carrier profile, driver direct phone number, and scheduled pickup appointment window.
04
Intake

Vehicle Pickup & BOL Inspection

The driver contacts you 2 hours prior to arrival. A joint walkaround inspection records pre-existing conditions on the digital Bill of Lading (BOL). You sign off, release the keys, and the vehicle is securely loaded.

Deliverable: Signed copy of origin Bill of Lading with documented vehicle condition photos.
05
Highway Transit

Interstate Transit & Telematics

The carrier navigates designated freight corridors. Highway checkpoint milestones and telematics updates post directly to your tracking dashboard. Our central dispatch actively monitors weather and traffic delays.

Deliverable: Live shipment portal tracking and 24-hour destination delivery ETA notice.
06
Destination

Delivery Inspection & Turnover

Carrier unloads the vehicle directly at your driveway or designated staging area. You conduct a final walkthrough matching the origin Bill of Lading, sign off the delivery receipt, and receive your keys.

Deliverable: Final delivery receipt countersigned, payment completed, and keys returned.
Readiness Guide

Vehicle Preparation Checklist

Follow these 8 essential steps before your carrier driver arrives to ensure a seamless loading experience.

Pre-Shipping Inspection Ready
Certified Driver Handover

1. Exterior Wash

Clean off dirt, mud, and dust so existing scratches or dings are visible during inspection.

2. 1/4 Fuel Tank

Keep approximately one-quarter tank of fuel. Extra fuel adds hazardous weight on multi-car trailers.

3. Remove Toll Passes

Remove or deactivate toll transponders (E-ZPass, SunPass) so highway scanners do not charge your account.

4. Clear Personal Cargo

Personal items are not covered by carrier insurance. Remove laptops, sports gear, and valuables.

5. Disable Alarms

Deactivate aftermarket anti-theft alarms or place in valet/transport mode to avoid dead batteries.

6. Check Fluid Leaks

Verify no active motor oil or coolant drips that could leak onto lower deck vehicles during transit.

7. Retract Accessories

Fold mirrors, retract antennas, and remove custom roof racks, bike racks, or loose spoilers.

8. One Working Key

Provide driver with one working ignition key or keyless entry fob for loading and unloading operations.
