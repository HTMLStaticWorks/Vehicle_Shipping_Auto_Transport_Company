Our Company Story

About RoadLink Auto

Founded on the principle that vehicle shipping should be predictable, transparent, and communication-driven from initial pickup to destination delivery.

Get a Transport Quote
Our Purpose

Redefining Vehicle Logistics Through Transparency

For decades, the auto transport industry struggled with erratic broker pricing, poor driver communication, and opaque tracking systems. RoadLink Auto was conceived to replace uncertainty with operational structure.

We operate as a centralized auto logistics coordinator linking private vehicle owners, dealership management teams, and relocation specialists with an audited network of licensed commercial carriers. By focusing on verified cargo insurance and direct driver contact, we eliminate the friction commonly found in vehicle shipping.

Our Mission

To deliver seamless vehicle transportation across every North American freight corridor through clear scheduling, transparent pricing, and proactive shipment communication.

Guiding Standards

Our Operating Principles

The core tenets that guide every carrier booking and driver dispatch.

Carrier Accountability

We mandate continuous monitoring of carrier DOT authority, safety audits, and active primary cargo liability policies before placing any vehicle.

Price Integrity

Our quotes are all-inclusive. We never quote artificially depressed bait rates only to demand surprise fuel or terminal surcharges later.

Proactive Communication

From 24-hour pickup warnings to highway milestone checkpoint alerts, customers always know where their vehicle is and who is driving it.

Coordination Model

Nationwide Terminal & Route Hub Network

Auto transport requires careful corridor routing. Rather than sending haulers down unverified detours, our central logistics desk coordinates carrier movement through designated staging hubs in major metropolitan regions.

This approach minimizes highway dwell times, ensures access to emergency roadside maintenance, and provides convenient backup transfer lots if a customer's neighborhood street has low power lines or truck weight restrictions.
